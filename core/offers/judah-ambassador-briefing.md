# Spark Sites - Ambassador Briefing (Judah)

What we sell, how it's tiered, and how you get paid. Built from the 8/29 conversation.

## How the ladder works
Every category has an entry tier (easy first yes), a growth tier (recurring revenue - the real business), and a premium tier. Your job is the introduction and the warm handoff; Grant closes with you shadowing until you're ready to run the close yourself.

## Small Business & Contractors (websites + marketing)

**Tier 1 - Get Online.** Two doors: a DIY website we host and support at **$34/mo**, or we build it for them - typically **$1,800 one-time + $34/mo** hosting. Contractors and small businesses buy the same way.

**Tier 2 - Spark Care Growth, $297/mo.** The flagship. We become their marketing foundation: we manage their site, monitor their Google Business Profile, set up their email campaigns (MailChimp or their CRM), set up their Google ads and Facebook ads and monitor them, install their Google Analytics and Meta pixels, and report monthly. They should plan on 6-12 months; 3 months prepaid is the preferred start. (Ongoing hands-on ad management beyond monitoring is a separate add-on.)

**Tier 3 - Marketing-Ready E-commerce, from $3,500 + $297/mo.** A full online store plus the whole marketing-ready stack, graduating into Growth.

## AI Training & Enablement

**Tier 1 - AI Onboarding Training, $450** (3 payments of $150). Three sessions: what AI is, how to make it do real work in their business, and their ongoing AI rhythm.

**Tier 2 - AI-First Roadmap + First System, $2,500.** We build one working AI system for their business and hand them the roadmap to becoming an AI-first company.

**Tier 3 - AI-First Organization, custom from $10K.** Team training, org-wide onboarding, full system build-out.

## Paid Public Speaking (your beachhead: universities / student activities)

**Tier 1 - Campus Talk, $500.** Grant speaks at their school.

**Tier 2 - Campus Impact Day, $1,000.** The talk, plus a leadership-team Q&A/mastermind, plus leave-behinds (10 copies of the goal-setting book, or the deck/an AI synthesis handout for anyone who couldn't attend). They get recording and reshare rights, and we collab-post the event on our channels - their event reaches beyond their campus.

**Tier 3 - Content + Leadership Intensive, $2,500-$3,000.** Everything in Tier 2 plus a recording team on site, custom-edited social posts, a leadership training block, and a curated student Q&A. The event becomes a semester of content for them.

## Your commission
Commission structure is in the shared Google Sheet ("Judah" tab) - rates are being finalized by Grant. Working model: a percentage of one-time revenue plus a percentage of the first months of recurring revenue on every deal you source that closes. Full commission on closed leads, as agreed 8/29.

## Who does what
- **You:** networking, introductions, university outreach, front-facing representation, shadowing Grant.
- **Grant:** closes (with you in the room), delivery oversight, your training.
- **Amber (Production Manager):** quality checks and delivery on every build.
