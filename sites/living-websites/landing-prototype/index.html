Living Websites by Spark Sites

Your website is alive. It gets better every week, on its own.

Most websites are finished the day they launch. Yours watches its own visitors, tests one improvement at a time, keeps what brings in more leads, and reports back to you.

How it works

  1. It watches.
  2. It tests one thing.
  3. It keeps the winner.
  4. It tells you what changed.

Roots

It drinks from your real numbers.

Roots reach down to the places your customers leave signals: your analytics, your ad accounts, your forms and your phone line. Every change it makes starts from what those sources show.

Branching

One test at a time. Only the stronger branch grows.

The site grows a second branch with one change on it. Visitors see both. When the evidence is in, the weaker branch is pruned and the stronger one leafs out.

  • AThe version you have now.
  • BThe challenger, with one change.

Growth rings

Every change leaves a ring.

Cut the trunk and you can read the site's history. Each ring is one evolution, with a date and what changed, kept in a public log you can open.
