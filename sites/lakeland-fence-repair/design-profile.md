# Design profile: Mossy Oak Fences (reference) -> Lakeland Fence Repair

Reference: https://www.mossyoakfences.com/ (Elementor site, Central Florida fence contractor). Profiled 2026-07-19 at Grant's direction: keep our palette, adopt their structure and visual energy, hero NOT full-vh, form like theirs, mega-footer with page hierarchy.

## What makes the reference feel designed (not template)

1. Compact hero: H1 + phone CTA + estimate CTA over imagery, height contained, business immediately clear.
2. Trust/award strip right under the hero (social proof before anything else).
3. Services and fence styles as IMAGE CARDS in big grids - photography carries the visual load.
4. Icon/number rows: 4 value props, 4-step "Ready to get started?" process with numbered steps.
5. Quote form is ON the home page, paired with the process steps and a phone number, not hidden on /contact.
6. Long-form SEO editorial blocks live lower on the page, after the visual sections.
7. Mega-footer: full page hierarchy (services, company, contact), address, phone, socials, legal row.
