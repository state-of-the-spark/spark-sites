1 · The Lockup

The primary mark is the wordmark lockup: the line-drawn fence glyph, a hairline divider, and the business name set in Marcellus. It is quiet, typographic, and reads "estate," not "lawn service." No filled badge shapes, no illustration.

Clear space & rules

  • Clear space on all sides: one picket height (the glyph's center picket).
  • Glyph color is forest #1F4D2E on light grounds, estate gold #B08D3F on charcoal. Never any other color.
  • Never add drop shadows, gradients, outlines, or containers around the lockup.
  • Never set the wordmark in another typeface or re-space it.

4 · Voice & Applications

Repair-first honesty: if it can be fixed, we fix it. Copy is plain, short, and never shouts. No exclamation marks, no invented awards or ratings.

SAY
Storm-bent fence? Most get repaired, not replaced.
If the fence is solid, tell a neighbor.
We will make it right.
DON'T SAY
#1 rated! Best in Florida!
CALL NOW — limited time!
Any claim we can't prove.

Application quick rules

  • Business card: charcoal face with gold ring monogram; cream face for details.
  • Yard sign: cream field, charcoal keyline — estate gate plaque, not coroplast.
  • Truck decal: lockup + phone + domain in cut vinyl, three-second read.
  • Photos: corner watermark, charcoal scrim at 78%, glyph + domain only.
  • Seal: hairline seal for stationery, review card, blind-emboss / foil.
