- **Getting to know you**

  - How did you hear about us?

    - Did anyone refer us?

  - What is your goal?

    - What's the top call to action?

    - Who are your target clients? Do they have a niche or psychographic?

    - Will you need clients to make payments on your website?

      - How many products do you have?

      - Do they have variations? (Different colors? Different sizes?)

- **Current State of things**

  - Do you have a current website?

    - What has your experience been with other designers so far?

    - Do you have logins to that service?

  - Do you own a domain name yet?

    - If so, where does it live? Where is it hosted?

    - Do you have the logins to that service?

  - Do you have email addresses yet?

    - If so, where do they live?

    - Where do you check your emails?

  - Do you have a brand?

    - Do you have a logo?

    - What are your brand colors?

    - Do you have a brand guide?

  - Do you have a Google Business listing?

    - How healthy is that?

  - Have you done any paid ads?

    - Facebook?

    - Google?

    - What has been your experience? Have you gotten any leads?

- **Marketing**

  - What have you done for marketing so far?

  - What is your brand promise?

  - What is your message?

  - What has worked best?

  - Do you have any landing pages or client funnels?

  - How is your social presence?

    - What is your handle?

    - What are your content pillars?

    - Have you successfully worked with influencers yet?

    - What is your strongest social platform?

    - What is your vision for your social presence next?

- **Closing**

  - When do you need this website completed by?

    - Are there any deadlines driving this decision?

  - Is there any other functionality you need the website to perform?

    - Do you need any intake forms for your clients? (depends on industry)

    - Do you need any automation from the website to your sales process or other aspects of your business process?

  - What is most important to you for this website?

    - That it helps level up your brand or message, or reaches your target audience?

    - That it happens quickly? That it is affordable?
