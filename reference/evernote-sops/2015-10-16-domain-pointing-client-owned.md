- *MAKE SURE THAT YOU HAVE MIGRATED THE SITE.*
  - Make sure that you have checked and compared ALL of the settings.
- *Add Domain*
  - Go to MySparkedSite.com --> My Account --> Managed WordPress --> Manage --> Settings.
  - Click Add Domain.
  - Type in the name of the domain name we will be pointing.
  - You are looking for 3 green checks.
  - Once you have clicked save, in Settings, click DNS.
  - Copy the IP address to the right of the A Record.
- *Change the A Record Zone File*
  - Log into the clients current hosting account.
  - Browse to their Domains.
  - Look for the terms "A Record", "Zone File", or "DNS".
  - Click the Edit button to edit the IP address.
  - COPY THE CURRENT IP ADDRESS SOMEWHERE IN CASE YOU NEED TO CHANGE IT BACK.
  - Now, paste the new IP address from MYSPARKSITE.com into this location.
  - Click Save.
  - Wait up to 15 minutes.
- *If that doesn't work, contact me.*
