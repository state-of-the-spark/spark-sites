- **Purchase**
  - Open the client order in http://www.SparkMySite.com --> Woocommerce --> Subscriptions.
  - Go to http://www.MySparkSite.com.
  - Log in under our login.
  - Go to http://www.MySparkSite.com --> Domains --> Register Domains.
  - Search for the domain they requested.
  - Leave our default credit card (ending in 4016).
  - Click Purchase.
  - Wait 10 minutes for domains to populate.
- **Set-Up**
  - Log into our account on http://www.MySparkSite.com.
  - Go to My Account --> Hosting Gateway.
  - Browse to the website we are working on.
  - Click Settings.
  - Click Add Domain.
  - From the drop-down menu, select the main domain you just purchased.
  - Check the Make this the Primary Domain box.
  - Click Save.
- **Domain Forwarding if Necessary**
  - If more than one domain is purchased, browse to http://www.MySparkSite.com --> My Account --> Domain Gateway.
  - Click the secondary domains available.
  - When the forwarding pop-up box appears, type or paste the domain name you are forwarding to. Choose Temporary unless you know for certain it is Permanent.
  - Click Save. Wait 10 minutes. Test each domain.
