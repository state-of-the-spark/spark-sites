Watch this 2 minute video from GoDaddy. [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=37Y-LChcEzI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=37Y-LChcEzI)

- Unlock the Domain.
  - Log into where the domain is currently hosted.
  - Browse to the Domain Locking settings.
  - Change the settings to "Unlocked".
  - Wait 15+ minutes.
- Purchase the Transfer
  - Log into MySparkedSite.com
  - Domain Names --> Transfer Domain
- Begin Transfer
  - Browse to Domains --> Transfers
  - Select the Domain Transfer that appears.
  - Click the domain.
  - Follow the steps to initiate the transfer.
  - Check your email for the Transfer ID and Security code.
  - Enter those in the Domain Transfer screen.
- Get Codes from Current Registrar
  - Log into the registrar where the domain current resides.
  - Initiate the Transfer Process/EEP(EPP?) code.
  - Copy the Code.
  - Enter the Transfer Confirmation code.
  - Wait 4 - 9 days for the email which will confirm that the transfer is complete.
