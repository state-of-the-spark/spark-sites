Systems Audit Template
TASK | NOTES | CHECKED-OK | CHECKED-FIX | FINDINGS | DELEGATED TO | DATE COMPLETE | FURTHER ACTION
Outgoing Voicemail Message | Listen to all outgoing messages in each department. Include company cell phones including senior team members.
On Hold Message | Check hold messages in each department for clarity, relevance and annoyance factor.
Phone Routing Check | Dial into extensions for accuracy. Do the same for department routing.
Email Signature Audit | Check and update email signatures and links for accuracy. Consider changing closing salutation, pictures, designatory letters.
Vacation Responder | Template vacation/out of office responders to include answers to common questions and links to further help.
Support/Chat Line Audit | Create 3 common support scenarios and call into and/or begin a chat to test speed, politeness and accuracy of support staff.
Web/Contact form Audit | Fill web forms on all pages and check incoming results. Follow routing of customer contact through process.
Security Systems | Make sure video security is recording as expected. Check camera angles and coverage. Update security lighting as needed, test alarms. Purge codes and consider reissue for all relevant staff.
Web Purchases Test | Test purchase 3 common products. Include complex purchases. Make purposeful mistakes to test purchase form complexity.
