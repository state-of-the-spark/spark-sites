**Funnel stage:** Engagement (middle). Goal: comments, saves, shares, tags, profile/page interaction — warm the audience the Awareness campaign built, get them talking, teach enough that they trust us before the Conversion ask. Optimized for the Meta engagement objective.

## PRIMARY TEXT — Option A (listicle, save-worthy) — RECOMMENDED

**5 things AI can do for a small business right now — and 2 it can't (no matter what the hype says).**

Let me explain what that actually means, because most owners get this backwards.

Here's what AI is genuinely good at today:

1. Writing your first drafts; emails, blog posts, product descriptions. Not the final word, but a running start every single day.
2. Answering the same 20 customer questions you're tired of answering.
3. Turning one video into a week of social posts.
4. Reading through your spreadsheets and telling you what's actually going on.
5. Following up with leads so they don't go cold while you're busy doing the work.

And here's what it can't do, no matter what anyone sells you:

6. Replace your judgment about your own business.
7. Fix a broken process. AI just makes a broken process run faster.

That last one is why most people feel frustrated by AI. They bolt a shiny tool onto a messy workflow and wonder why nothing improved.

The goal is for you to understand this, not just trust us.

Which of these five would save you the most hours this week? Tell me in the comments; I read every one. 👇

## PRIMARY TEXT — Option B (myth-busting, contrarian)

**"AI is going to replace me." "AI is too complicated for a business my size." "AI is just a fad."**

I hear these every week. Let me clear up all three, because being frustrated by AI usually comes from believing one of them.

**Myth 1: It'll replace you.** It won't. AI is a force multiplier, not a replacement. It does the repetitive 80% so you can spend more real time with your clients and your team; that's the whole point.

**Myth 2: You're too small.** Backwards. Small businesses see the *fastest* wins, because you can actually change how you work without a 12-person committee signing off.

**Myth 3: It's a fad.** Your competitors down the street are already using it to answer leads faster and publish more. That's not a fad; that's a head start you can still catch.

High-end doesn't have to mean high-cost, and neither does getting started with AI.

So be honest with me: which myth has been holding *you* back? Drop the number (1, 2, or 3) below. 👇
