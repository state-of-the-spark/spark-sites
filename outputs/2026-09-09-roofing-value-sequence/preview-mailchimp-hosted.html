Day 35 · Industry insightThe mistake on 9 out of 10 roofing websitesPreheader: It's costing them calls right now.

Hi *|IF:MMERGE19|**|MMERGE19|**|ELSE:|*there*|END:IF|*,

Last insight in this batch, and it's the most common one.

Nine out of ten roofing sites I look at are built to talk about the company. "Family owned since." "We are committed to quality." All about them.
