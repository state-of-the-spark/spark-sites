# Retargeting Ad Copy Swipe File

> **When to use:** Every campaign needs a retargeting ad running alongside the cold ads. Use this swipe file when creating retargeting ads for people who visited the offer page (Skool about page, sales page, etc.) but didn't convert. Note: retargeting copy intentionally breaks the cold-traffic Deep Ad rules (direct questions, "you/your" address) - the audience is warm and already knows the offer.
>
> Source: Skool Ads Lab swipe file PDF, converted to markdown 2026-07-17. Content preserved as-is, not synthesized.

## How to Find the Objections

1. **Reach out to your current members (specific)**
   - "Hey [NAME], I'm curious: Before you joined [YOUR GROUP NAME], what was holding you back? And what finally convinced you to take the leap?"

2. **Bottom part of the value equation (general)**
   - Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice)
   - The bottom of the equation - Time Delay and Effort & Sacrifice - is where the objections live.

---

## Framework

### 1. Opening Approaches

- Direct acknowledgement: "You didn't join [community]?"
- Observation: "I saw you checking it out..."
- Disclaimer: "We cannot promise you the same results..."

### 2. Addressing Objections

- (these are just examples. You'll need to find your own client's objections)
- Budget concerns
- Time concerns
- Value concerns
- Technical ability concerns

### 3. Counterarguments

- "You don't need a huge budget..."
- "Everything is explained step-by-step..."
- "You don't need any prior experience..."
- "We offer a X-day money back guarantee..."

### 4. Social Proof

- Specific case studies with numbers
- Member success stories
- Before and after scenarios

### 5. Urgency Techniques

- Limited-time pricing
- Limited availability
- Fear of missing out

### 6. Risk Reversal

- Money-back guarantees
- Cancel anytime policies
- "Worst case vs. best case" framing

### 7. Call to Action

- Clear next steps
- Direct link
- "See you on the other side" closers
