# The Deep Ad Framework

**Source:** Skool Ads Lab (codified from Grant's course PDF, 2026-07-17).
**What it is:** The full spec behind the "Deep Ad" style (500-800 word Primary 1 in every static ads batch). Deep Ads are built to blend into the Facebook feed while delivering high impact - they read like a post, not an ad. This file is the source of truth for the hook rules already baked into SKILL.md and adds the parts the skill previously lacked: the five dimensions, the body structure, and the element brainstorm.

**When to load:** Every time a Deep Ad primary is written (static ads mode, Primary 1) and whenever reviewing Deep Ad copy.

---

## The Five Dimensions (Core of the Framework)

Incorporate as many of these dimensions as possible in every Deep Ad. The more dimensions present, the stronger the ad. Brainstorm options for each BEFORE writing copy.

| # | Dimension | Definition | Example |
|---|-----------|------------|---------|
| 1 | **Resonance** | Shared experience, identity, or common ground that creates a sense of "we." Aligns the ad with the group's lived experience. | "As e-commerce entrepreneurs, navigating the ever-changing digital landscape is a constant challenge." |
| 2 | **Pain** | The external SYMPTOM of a larger problem - the tangible experience, not the root cause. | Rising ad costs; can't reach followers; struggling to lose weight despite a strict plan |
| 3 | **Benefit** | The specific, tangible, measurable result that solves the pain and is directly attributable to the unique approach. | Steady conversions without constant discounts; finding high-ROI properties without hours of research |
| 4 | **Challenge** | Why other/existing solutions fail - the limitation that creates the opening for the unique approach. | "Hours in the gym"; "unsustainable strict diets"; "tools that only show national trends" |
| 5 | **Unique Approach** | The distinctive, NAMED solution that overcomes the challenges. Give it a scientific or innovative-sounding name for differentiation and authority. | "Deep" ads, P90X, 'Dynamic Customer Targeting', 'Micro-Market Analysis' |

### Secondary Qualifier Elements (add specificity and urgency)

| Element | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Time Frame | "In just 60 days" / "Within two weeks" |
| Year | "In 2026, here's how..." |
| Offer | "Access a free demo" / "Join an exclusive webinar" |
| Problem (root) | "The real reason ad performance declines" |
| Status Quo Solution | "Traditional ad strategies" |

## Non-Negotiable Rules

These can never be broken in a Deep Ad. (Rules 1-2 are already in SKILL.md hook rules; this is the complete set with the why.)

1. **No direct questions in hooks.** Questions feel intrusive and salesy and invite a binary "no." Use resonant statements instead: "Struggling with low ad engagement can feel frustrating and costly."
2. **No "you"/"your" in the first three lines.** Direct address triggers defensiveness. Use shared-experience language: "Improving ad strategies has been a challenge for many marketers."
3. **No avatar call-outs.** Never open with "Attention business owners!" - it flags the post as an ad. Prefer: "For business owners, navigating ad costs can be tricky."
4. **No overhyped language.** Ban "amazing," "unbelievable," "secret trick." Use authentic, conversational claims: "We've developed a method that consistently works."
5. **Inclusive, first-person language.** Write from "we," "I," or the Resonance group's perspective - never at the reader.
6. **Hook-body consistency.** Tone, message, and promise in the hook must carry through the body. Disconnection kills trust.

## Hook (First Three Lines / Default Viewable Text)

- **Length:** 123-135 characters (visible before "See more").
- **Job:** Lead with Resonance + multiple Pains, tease the Benefit without overselling, keep detail minimal so the reader clicks "See more." Concise yet detailed - no fluff.
- Example: "As marketers, facing skyrocketing ad costs and declining engagement is all too familiar... There's a method that changes everything."

### Hook Formulas (full versions)

The magic isn't the formula - it's incorporating LOTS of elements. Brainstorm elements first, then fill.

**Formula 1 - Transformation:**
"How [Resonance] go from [Pain 1], [Pain 2] to [Benefit 1], [Benefit 2] using [Unique Approach] in [Time Frame]."
- "How busy parents go from struggling with diets and lack of energy to sustainable weight loss using 'Interval Strength Training' in 12 weeks."

**Formula 2 - Even Without:**
"Here's how even [Resonance] (without [Challenge]) are [Benefit] using [Unique Approach]."
- "Here's how even new online stores (without big ad budgets) are increasing conversions using 'Dynamic Customer Targeting'."

**Formula 3 - Eliminates:**
"This [Unique Approach] eliminates [Pain 1], [Pain 2], even [Pain 3], without [Challenge]; no need to [Challenge 2], just [Unique Approach Details]."
- "This 'Interval Strength Training' eliminates long gym sessions, strict diets, and fatigue, without burnout; just 30 minutes a day."

Headlines use the same formulas compressed: "How 'Agency Growth Model' Helps Agencies Achieve Consistent Leads Without Burnout."

## Body Structure (Extended Text, after "See more")

Five steps, in order:

1. **Empathize with the audience.** Deeply describe MULTIPLE pains ("Investing time and money into ads that don't deliver... it can be disheartening"). Then build trust with credibility from lived experience ("As fellow marketers who've walked this path...").
2. **Introduce the solution.** Link it directly to the pains named in step 1, then highlight what makes it unique ("Unlike traditional methods, this approach adapts in real-time...").
3. **Unpack the benefits.** Create visualization scenarios ("Imagine campaigns that consistently bring in quality leads...") and name specific, measurable advantages.
4. **Address challenges and objections.** Name the common barriers (time, cost, complexity) and reassure ("Our system is user-friendly and cost-effective, designed with marketers in mind").
5. **Call to action.** Direct and clear - tell them exactly what to do next, and include the full clickable URL.

## Element Brainstorm (run before writing any Deep Ad)

Generate multiple options per element from the offer + audience reference files, then mix and match:

| Element | What It Is | Prompt |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Pains | Summarized problems / symptoms | What tangible frustrations does the audience feel weekly? |
| Internal Conflict | Feelings caused by the pains | Frustrated, overwhelmed, doubting, afraid of falling behind? |
| Benefits | Desired external results | What measurable outcomes do they want? |
| Internal Benefits | Feelings from achieving benefits | Confidence, peace of mind, relief, empowerment? |
| Ultimate Benefits | The deeper reasons | Financial freedom, time, scaling, family? |
| Challenges | Problems with other solutions | Why did what they tried before fail? |
| Solution | Type of product/service | What is the offer, plainly? |
| Unique Approach | Named differentiator | What proprietary name captures the mechanism? |
| Resonance | Group / shared identity | What "we" does this audience belong to? |

Note the layering: Pain → Internal Conflict → Benefit → Internal Benefit → Ultimate Benefit is a depth ladder. Deep Ads earn their name by descending it.

## Performance Guidelines

- **Blend in naturally.** The ad should feel like organic feed content. No flashy graphics or text-heavy creative.
- **Simple visuals.** Clean, high-quality images that match the message. Less is more.
- **Consistency everywhere.** Primary text, headline, visual, landing page, and offer must carry one unified message through the whole funnel.
- **Connection over sale.** Salesy language breaks the spell; the ad's job is relationship first.
- **Pre-qualify with engagement.** A Deep Ad works when people READ it. Watch CTR ratios: overall CTR should run higher than link CTR - that gap means people are engaging with the copy.
- **Test and refine.** Multiple element combinations, data-driven iteration.

## Integration with This Skill

- **Primary 1 in every static-ads concept IS a Deep Ad** (500-800 words). Apply this framework fully there. Other primaries (UGC/Native, Direct Response, Pattern Interrupt, Testimonial) inherit the non-negotiable rules but not the 5-step body structure.
- **Before writing:** run the Element Brainstorm from the resolved `offer.md` + `audience.md` + `angles/`. The selected angle supplies the psychological entry; the dimensions supply the raw material.
- **Quality gate:** before saving a batch, check each Deep Ad names all five dimensions - Resonance, Pain (plural), Benefit, Challenge, Unique Approach. A Deep Ad missing its named Unique Approach is the most common failure; Spark offers should use named mechanisms (e.g., the SPARK Method, Starter Site) rather than generic descriptions.
- **Compliance still wins:** dimension language must survive the 6-lens review (FTC, Meta Personal Attributes, substantiation). "Even without [Challenge]" framings must not assert personal attributes in Special Ad Categories.
